Illustrative stories showing the journey Work9ja is built to deliver.

Your identity and CV are not a side note.

Work9ja handles real identities, employment histories and payments — so security and Nigerian data-protection compliance are built in, not bolted on.

Row-level data isolation

Every record is access-scoped and tested for cross-user and cross-company leaks. You see your applications; recruiters see only theirs.

Private, signed storage

CVs and documents live in private buckets with short-lived signed links — never a public URL anyone can guess.

Verified employers

Employer verification, scam-job reporting and moderation keep fake postings off the platform.

Secure payments

Server-verified, idempotent billing with secure webhooks — no privileged keys ever touch the browser.

Admin MFA & audit logs

Multi-factor for administrators, full audit trails, backups and tested recovery drills.

Consent & deletion

Clear privacy notices, explicit consent before AI processes your data, and real data-deletion workflows.
